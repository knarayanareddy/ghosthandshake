THE SKETCHBOOK ODYSSEY — narration script
Voice: storybook narrator (voice-00). Timings refine during assembly.

#  ID    LINE
1  N01   Tell us, O Pencil, of the boat of many folds — the sketch who saw the desk's far edge, and came home to be kept.
2  N02   Every night, the Hand filled the pages with a world. Then he would yawn... and forget it.
3  N03   This is Scrib. First of all doodles. Captain of nothing... yet.
4  N04   But mornings bring gods. And gods bring... tidying.
5  N05   Hatch had been there since the very first page. Now, half of him... wasn't.
6  N06   Beyond the Desk Sea, whispered the Cup, there is a Last Page. What is kept... is never lost.
7  N07   So the notebook tipped its spine — and the first voyage ever sailed on a desk began.
8  N08   First came the Fan — wrath of the winds. Hold fast, little crew. Hold FAST!
9  N09   Some heroes roar. Their monsters simply... anchor.
10 N10   Draw faster than the erasing. That was the whole plan. It was a terrible plan. It was the only plan.
11 N11   At the heart of the whirlpool lived a loneliness bigger than any storm. They promised her: we'll stir again someday.
12 N12   Beware the Beach of the Radio — where the dancing never, ever stops.
13 N13   One note rang out like a bell... and the crew remembered their heading.
14 N14   The toll at the Strait of the Couch was simple: one hug. No one had ever offered. Everyone paid.
15 N15   But dusk came early. Gods keep short hours. And the Eraser keeps none.
16 N16   What is half-erased? Neither here... nor there.
17 N17   Only the Bird could still fly. So she carried what was left of their captain... up toward the light.
18 N18   The Hand knelt. And gods, it turns out... can cry.
19 N19   In the drawer lay a book older than the notebook — every drawing he had ever loved, kept. He had been a boy whose world was erased too.
20 N20   He framed them. He painted them. And color came to the Desk like a sunrise that had waited years for its cue.
21 N21   Scrib looked back once. Captains do. Then he sailed past the edge of the page — into a sea the size of everything else.
22 N22   Nothing drawn with love is ever lost. It only waits for the next hand.
23 N23   The Sketchbook Odyssey. ...For whoever comes next.
