What this is

A secret-word ritual, settled on-chain.

01 · THE RITUALTwo strangers, one word.

You and a partner each pick one of eight words without telling the other. If you picked the same one, the chain will know — and so will you, instantly.

02 · THE CHAINCommit–reveal on Monad.

Your word is hashed on Monad Testnet before reveal. Nobody — not you, not the builder, not the network — can read it or change it in between.

03 · THE WALLEvery bond draws a glyph.

Each match adds one permanent, deterministic stroke to a shared canvas called Afterimage. It only grows. It never erases.

The whole game

Five moves. That’s the entire thing.

MOVE 1Pick a word

One of eight. In your head. Say nothing.

MOVE 2Commit

Your word’s hash goes on-chain. The word itself goes nowhere.

MOVE 3Partner picks

They connect, pick their own word — hopefully the same one.

MOVE 4Both reveal

Within ~10 minutes of your commit. Second reveal closes the bond.

MOVE 5Bond + glyph

Flash on your screen, name on the leaderboard, new stroke on the wall.

Total cost per round: a few dollars’ worth of MON testnet gas. Total time: under three minutes.

Before you play · ~2 minutes

Two things in your pocket.

  • A wallet — MetaMask (browser or phone) works. Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, any EVM wallet.
  • Some MON — testnet only, zero value, from faucet.monad.xyz or the event claim at Devnads. Ten MON is a lifetime supply.
  • The network — you don’t set this up. Pressing Connect adds Monad Testnet (chain 10143) to your wallet automatically.

Eight words, deliberately few: in a room of forty people, somebody will pick what you picked. You just have to find them — or let them find you.

Tap a tile to select (it inverts). Your choice lives only in this browser tab — it never touches the network until you press Commit.

Step 3 · Commit

Your word goes into a hash. Only the hash goes on-chain.

  • Press Commit. Your wallet asks to sign one transaction (~0.01 MON).
  • The log says committed fog (keep this phone) — the secret is stored in this browser only (localStorage). A different phone can’t reveal for you.
  • A clock starts: the reveal window is ~10 minutes (2,000 blocks at ~300–400 ms each).
# in your browser, right now
salt  = randomBytes(32)          // 256 bits of luck
hash  = keccak256(word ‖ salt)   // packed
commit(hash)  ──────────────►  Monad

# on-chain, permanently
commitments[you] = hash
# the word itself: nowhere. not in the
# tx, not in the event, not recoverable
# by anyone before reveal.

Step 4 · The reveal

Tell them you picked a word. Not which one.

YOU“I committed.”

Your partner — the stranger you suspect shares your word — connects, picks their word, commits. They’re under the same ~10-minute window from their commit.

BOTHPress Reveal.

Reveal sends your word + salt. The contract recomputes keccak(word‖salt) and checks it against your commit. Wrong word? It reverts. That’s the whole trick.

THENFirst waits, second closes.

The first person to reveal parks on-chain and waits. The second matching reveal mints the pair, fires Matched, and both screens light up.

Miss the window? Your reveal reverts with a clear log line — you just commit fresh and go again. Nothing is lost but a little gas.

Step 5 · The match

This is the moment.

You matched with 0x0aC7…ae1c… LOG → MATCH #3 0x94CA… × 0x0aC7… LEADERBOARD → both addresses tick up to 1 bond

No confetti, no fanfare — a 150 ms ease, then the fact of it. You now know this stranger’s word was yours. The chain has the timestamp.

# Matched event, on-chain
Matched(
  a        = 0x94CA…7bB1,
  b        = 0x0aC7…ae1c,
  wordHash = 0x9f1c…44aa,   // fog
  pairIndex= 3,
  atBlock  = 57944801
)

Afterimage · the wall

Every bond leaves a mark.

Afterimage6 strangers, 3 bonds.

Open /canvas.html — no wallet, no login. Each bond draws one stroke whose position and colour are deterministic from the pair (same two addresses, same word, same block → same stroke, on every screen, forever). The picture only grows.

When things go wrong

Every failure says exactly what it means.

no local secret — commit on this deviceYou pressed Reveal in a browser that never committed.Commit here first — the secret lives in the browser that made it.
commit reverted on chain — nothing was set. try again.You already have an active commitment (one per address).Wait it out, Cancel after the window, or use a fresh wallet.
reveal reverted on chain — window expired, or the secret is not on this device.More than ~10 min (2000 blocks) passed since commit, or the word/salt don’t match.Commit fresh and reveal quickly. It’s one small transaction.
cancel reverted — the window may still be open. wait, then try again.Cancel only unlocks after the reveal window closes.Wait a few minutes and retry — or just let it expire naturally.

Watch the LOG panel: every step, hash and outcome is written there. Reverts cost gas (Monad bills gas_limit × price), which is why the app checks every receipt instead of pretending success.

Under the hood · for the technicals

Small on purpose.

commit(bytes32)      # 1 active commitment / address
reveal(string,bytes32)# checks keccak(word‖salt)
cancel(address)      # permissionless, post-window

REVEAL_WINDOW = 2000 blocks   # strict >: +2000 still valid
no block.timestamp     # Monad executes async
no external calls      # no reentrancy, ever
